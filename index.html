About LED Talk

LED Talk is a simple LED message board app that lets you type messages and display them in a bright, bold, eye-catching style.

  • Custom text and background colors
  • Glow and motion effects
  • Adjustable text size
  • Quick messages and recent messages
  • Emoji support

Privacy Notice

LED Talk is designed with simplicity and privacy in mind.

  • No account required: You do not need to sign up or log in to use the app.
  • No personal data collection: LED Talk does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.
  • Local usage only: Messages and recent content used in the app are intended for use on your device.
  • No selling of personal information: We do not sell or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

If future versions of the app include additional features that affect data handling, this page may be updated accordingly.
